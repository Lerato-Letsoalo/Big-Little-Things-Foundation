FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I become a volunteer?

Click the Register as Volunteer button on our Volunteer page. Fill in your details, and you'll receive a confirmation once your account is approved. You can then log in and register for upcoming events.

Is there a minimum age to volunteer?

Volunteers under 18 are welcome with parental consent. Please contact us for a consent form before registering.

How are donations used?

Every donation goes directly to our community drives — food parcels, clothing, sanitary products, and stationery. Administrative costs are kept to an absolute minimum.

Can I donate items instead of money?

Yes! We welcome food, clothing, toiletries, and sanitary products. Use the Donate page to submit a physical donation and we'll arrange collection or drop-off.

How do I get my volunteer certificate?

After each volunteering session, an administrator approves your attendance. Your personalised PDF certificate is then automatically available in your Volunteer Dashboard under "My Certificates".

How can my company partner with you?

We welcome corporate partnerships of all sizes — from sponsoring a specific drive to ongoing support. Reach out using the form above and mention "Partnership" as the subject.
